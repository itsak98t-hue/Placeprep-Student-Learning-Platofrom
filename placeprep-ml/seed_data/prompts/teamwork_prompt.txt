Create behavioral interview dataset entries for the "teamwork" category.

The goal is to generate realistic teamwork-related interview responses for the PlacePrep behavioral evaluation dataset.

Use this exact JSON schema for every entry:
{
  "id": integer,
  "question": string,
  "category": "teamwork",
  "answer": string,
  "label": "weak" | "average" | "strong",
  "score_clarity": integer,
  "score_structure": integer,
  "score_impact": integer,
  "missing": [string, ...]
}

Question style:
- Use realistic prompts such as:
  - "Tell me about a time you worked effectively in a team."
  - "Describe a group project that required strong collaboration."
  - "Tell me about a time you helped your team succeed."

Category-specific realism:
- Focus on collaboration, role sharing, coordination, helping teammates, and working through dependency issues.
- Strong answers should show collaboration plus individual contribution and a clear team outcome.
- Weak answers may be too generic, focus only on "we" without personal contribution, or skip results.
- Average answers may be decent but still vague about ownership or impact.

Realism rules:
- Include weak, average, and strong answers.
- Include short, medium, and long answers.
- Some answers should feel natural and slightly messy.
- Do not make every response polished.
- Keep examples believable for student, campus, internship, and junior work settings.

Scoring guidance:
- weak:
  - clarity 2-4
  - structure 2-4
  - impact 1-3
- average:
  - clarity 5-7
  - structure 4-6
  - impact 4-6
- strong:
  - clarity 7-10
  - structure 7-10
  - impact 7-10

Missing-field guidance:
- Use realistic missing items such as:
  - "individual contribution"
  - "specific actions"
  - "team coordination details"
  - "result"
  - "metrics"
  - "reflection"

Formatting rules:
- Output valid JSON only.
- Keep category exactly "teamwork".
- Ensure every record includes all fields.
- Ensure "missing" is always an array.
- Do not include explanations outside the JSON.
