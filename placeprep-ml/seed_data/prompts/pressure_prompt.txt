Create behavioral interview dataset entries for the "pressure" category.

The goal is to generate realistic pressure-related interview responses for the PlacePrep behavioral evaluation dataset.

Use this exact JSON schema for every entry:
{
  "id": integer,
  "question": string,
  "category": "pressure",
  "answer": string,
  "label": "weak" | "average" | "strong",
  "score_clarity": integer,
  "score_structure": integer,
  "score_impact": integer,
  "missing": [string, ...]
}

Question style:
- Use realistic prompts such as:
  - "Tell me about a time you worked under pressure."
  - "Describe a high-pressure situation and how you handled it."
  - "Tell me about a time you managed tight deadlines."

Category-specific realism:
- Focus on deadlines, competing priorities, unexpected blockers, urgent requests, or exam/project crunch periods.
- Strong answers should show prioritization, composure, decision-making, and outcome.
- Weak answers may sound reactive, disorganized, or vague about what was done.
- Average answers may show effort but not strong structure or impact.

Realism rules:
- Include weak, average, and strong answers.
- Include short, medium, and long answers.
- Some answers should feel rushed or conversational while still believable.
- Do not make every response polished.
- Keep scenarios realistic for students, interns, and early-career roles.

Scoring guidance:
- weak:
  - clarity 2-4
  - structure 2-4
  - impact 1-3
- average:
  - clarity 5-7
  - structure 4-6
  - impact 4-6
- strong:
  - clarity 7-10
  - structure 7-10
  - impact 7-10

Missing-field guidance:
- Use realistic missing items such as:
  - "prioritization details"
  - "specific actions"
  - "result"
  - "metrics"
  - "reflection"
  - "decision rationale"

Formatting rules:
- Output valid JSON only.
- Keep category exactly "pressure".
- Ensure every record includes all fields.
- Ensure "missing" is always an array.
- Do not include explanations outside the JSON.
