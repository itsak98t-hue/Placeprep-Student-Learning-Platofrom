Behavioral label guidance:

weak
- vague or generic
- unclear ownership
- weak STAR structure
- little or no measurable impact
- often missing context, actions, or results
- score ranges:
  clarity 2-4
  structure 2-4
  impact 1-3

average
- understandable answer with some structure
- some ownership shown
- some result, but limited specificity or metrics
- may miss depth, reflection, or stronger business impact
- score ranges:
  clarity 5-7
  structure 4-6
  impact 4-6

strong
- clear and specific
- strong ownership and concrete actions
- visible STAR-like structure
- includes outcome, impact, and ideally metrics
- score ranges:
  clarity 7-10
  structure 7-10
  impact 7-10
