Create behavioral interview dataset entries for the "conflict" category.

The goal is to generate realistic responses to conflict-related interview questions for the PlacePrep behavioral evaluation dataset.

Use this exact JSON schema for every entry:
{
  "id": integer,
  "question": string,
  "category": "conflict",
  "answer": string,
  "label": "weak" | "average" | "strong",
  "score_clarity": integer,
  "score_structure": integer,
  "score_impact": integer,
  "missing": [string, ...]
}

Question style:
- Use realistic conflict questions such as:
  - "Tell me about a conflict you had with a teammate."
  - "Describe a time you disagreed with someone on your team."
  - "Tell me about a difficult interpersonal situation and how you handled it."
- Keep the question phrasing varied.

Category-specific realism:
- Focus on disagreements, misunderstandings, ownership clashes, communication gaps, or role confusion.
- Strong answers should show calm handling, listening, resolution steps, and team-focused outcomes.
- Weak answers may sound defensive, blame others, avoid ownership, or skip the resolution.
- Average answers may describe the situation but lack depth, reflection, or measurable outcome.

Realism rules:
- Include weak, average, and strong answers.
- Include short, medium, and long answers.
- Some answers should be slightly messy and conversational.
- Do not make every answer polished.
- Keep the examples believable for student, intern, or early-career candidates.

Scoring guidance:
- weak:
  - clarity 2-4
  - structure 2-4
  - impact 1-3
- average:
  - clarity 5-7
  - structure 4-6
  - impact 4-6
- strong:
  - clarity 7-10
  - structure 7-10
  - impact 7-10

Missing-field guidance:
- Use realistic missing items such as:
  - "ownership"
  - "resolution details"
  - "specific actions"
  - "result"
  - "reflection"
  - "stakeholder management"

Formatting rules:
- Output valid JSON only.
- Keep category exactly "conflict".
- Ensure every record includes all fields.
- Ensure "missing" is always an array.
- Do not include explanations outside the JSON.
