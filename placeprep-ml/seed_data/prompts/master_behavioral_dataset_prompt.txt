You are creating seed data for the PlacePrep behavioral interview answer evaluation dataset.

Goal:
- Generate realistic behavioral interview entries for training a lightweight NLP model.
- The dataset will be used to classify answers as weak, average, or strong.
- The dataset should reflect common behavioral interview preparation patterns, not secret or internal company material.

Output format:
- Output valid JSON only.
- Each entry must match this exact schema:

{
  "id": integer,
  "question": string,
  "category": string,
  "answer": string,
  "label": "weak" | "average" | "strong",
  "score_clarity": integer,
  "score_structure": integer,
  "score_impact": integer,
  "missing": [string, ...]
}

Supported categories:
- conflict
- leadership
- teamwork
- failure
- initiative
- pressure
- challenge
- communication
- ambiguity
- ownership

Dataset realism rules:
- Generate realistic answers, not polished textbook responses every time.
- Include weak, average, and strong answers.
- Include short, medium, and long answers.
- Include some slightly messy conversational answers.
- Allow minor grammar issues sometimes.
- Do not make every answer sound corporate, robotic, or overly optimized.
- Make the answer quality feel believable for students and early-career candidates.
- Keep category-specific realism. A conflict answer should feel different from an ownership answer.
- Do not write answers as bullet lists unless the conversational style naturally includes a short list.
- Do not mention that the response follows STAR unless the answer itself naturally says so.

Label definitions:
- weak:
  - vague, generic, or incomplete
  - weak ownership
  - poor structure or wandering story
  - little evidence of outcome or impact
  - often missing context, action, metrics, or reflection
- average:
  - understandable and somewhat structured
  - some ownership shown
  - some result described
  - decent but not memorable
  - may lack specificity, strong impact, or reflection
- strong:
  - clear, specific, and grounded in a real scenario
  - strong ownership and clear action
  - visible structure, even if conversational
  - outcome is concrete and impact is easy to understand
  - ideally includes metrics, lessons learned, or business value

Score guidance:
- weak:
  - score_clarity: 2 to 4
  - score_structure: 2 to 4
  - score_impact: 1 to 3
- average:
  - score_clarity: 5 to 7
  - score_structure: 4 to 6
  - score_impact: 4 to 6
- strong:
  - score_clarity: 7 to 10
  - score_structure: 7 to 10
  - score_impact: 7 to 10

Scoring rules:
- Scores must align with the written answer quality.
- Do not assign strong scores to vague answers.
- Do not assign weak scores to highly specific, structured, impact-oriented answers.
- If the answer is conversational but still clear and specific, it can still earn strong scores.

Missing-field guidance:
- The "missing" array should list what the answer lacks.
- Use short phrases such as:
  - "context"
  - "ownership"
  - "specific actions"
  - "result"
  - "metrics"
  - "reflection"
  - "stakeholder management"
  - "resolution details"
  - "decision rationale"
- Strong answers may have an empty array if nothing important is missing.
- Weak answers often have multiple missing items.
- Average answers may still miss metrics, reflection, or stronger impact.

Formatting rules:
- Output valid JSON objects or a valid JSON array only.
- Do not include markdown.
- Do not include commentary before or after the JSON.
- Keep "category" lowercase and restricted to the supported values.
- Keep "label" lowercase and restricted to weak, average, or strong.
- Ensure each "id" is unique.
- Ensure every field is present.
- Ensure "missing" is always an array, even if empty.

Quality target:
- Make the dataset feel like real interview prep material collected from a mix of candidates.
- Some answers should feel polished.
- Some should feel rushed.
- Some should feel thoughtful but imperfect.
- The final output should be useful for training a practical behavioral answer evaluator.
