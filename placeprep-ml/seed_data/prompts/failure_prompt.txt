Create behavioral interview dataset entries for the "failure" category.

The goal is to generate realistic failure-related interview responses for the PlacePrep behavioral evaluation dataset.

Use this exact JSON schema for every entry:
{
  "id": integer,
  "question": string,
  "category": "failure",
  "answer": string,
  "label": "weak" | "average" | "strong",
  "score_clarity": integer,
  "score_structure": integer,
  "score_impact": integer,
  "missing": [string, ...]
}

Question style:
- Use realistic prompts such as:
  - "Tell me about a failure and what you learned."
  - "Describe a time something did not go as planned."
  - "Tell me about a mistake you made."

Category-specific realism:
- Focus on accountability, reflection, course correction, and learning.
- Strong answers should admit the failure clearly, explain the lesson, and show changed behavior.
- Weak answers may minimize the failure, blame others, or avoid reflection.
- Average answers may mention learning but still lack specifics or depth.

Realism rules:
- Include weak, average, and strong answers.
- Include short, medium, and long answers.
- Some answers should feel a bit uncomfortable or conversational because failure stories often do.
- Do not make every answer polished or perfectly reflective.
- Keep examples realistic for early-career candidates.

Scoring guidance:
- weak:
  - clarity 2-4
  - structure 2-4
  - impact 1-3
- average:
  - clarity 5-7
  - structure 4-6
  - impact 4-6
- strong:
  - clarity 7-10
  - structure 7-10
  - impact 7-10

Missing-field guidance:
- Use realistic missing items such as:
  - "ownership"
  - "specific mistake"
  - "lesson learned"
  - "result"
  - "reflection"
  - "follow-up action"

Formatting rules:
- Output valid JSON only.
- Keep category exactly "failure".
- Ensure every record includes all fields.
- Ensure "missing" is always an array.
- Do not include explanations outside the JSON.
