Create behavioral interview dataset entries for the "communication" category.

The goal is to generate realistic communication-related interview responses for the PlacePrep behavioral evaluation dataset.

Use this exact JSON schema for every entry:
{
  "id": integer,
  "question": string,
  "category": "communication",
  "answer": string,
  "label": "weak" | "average" | "strong",
  "score_clarity": integer,
  "score_structure": integer,
  "score_impact": integer,
  "missing": [string, ...]
}

Question style:
- Use realistic prompts such as:
  - "Tell me about a time you had to communicate something difficult."
  - "Describe a time you explained a complex idea clearly."
  - "Tell me about a situation where communication was critical."

Category-specific realism:
- Focus on explaining clearly, updating stakeholders, simplifying complexity, giving feedback, or avoiding misunderstandings.
- Strong answers should show audience awareness, clarity, adaptation, and outcome.
- Weak answers may be generic, overly abstract, or skip what was actually communicated.
- Average answers may be understandable but not especially thoughtful or impactful.

Realism rules:
- Include weak, average, and strong answers.
- Include short, medium, and long answers.
- Some answers should sound conversational and slightly rough.
- Do not make every answer polished.
- Keep examples realistic for peer teams, professors, mentors, managers, and cross-functional contexts.

Scoring guidance:
- weak:
  - clarity 2-4
  - structure 2-4
  - impact 1-3
- average:
  - clarity 5-7
  - structure 4-6
  - impact 4-6
- strong:
  - clarity 7-10
  - structure 7-10
  - impact 7-10

Missing-field guidance:
- Use realistic missing items such as:
  - "audience adaptation"
  - "specific actions"
  - "stakeholder management"
  - "result"
  - "clarity details"
  - "feedback loop"

Formatting rules:
- Output valid JSON only.
- Keep category exactly "communication".
- Ensure every record includes all fields.
- Ensure "missing" is always an array.
- Do not include explanations outside the JSON.
