Create behavioral interview dataset entries for the "initiative" category.

The goal is to generate realistic initiative-related interview responses for the PlacePrep behavioral evaluation dataset.

Use this exact JSON schema for every entry:
{
  "id": integer,
  "question": string,
  "category": "initiative",
  "answer": string,
  "label": "weak" | "average" | "strong",
  "score_clarity": integer,
  "score_structure": integer,
  "score_impact": integer,
  "missing": [string, ...]
}

Question style:
- Use realistic prompts such as:
  - "Tell me about a time you took initiative."
  - "Describe a situation where you went beyond your assigned work."
  - "Tell me about a time you proactively solved a problem."

Category-specific realism:
- Focus on proactive behavior, noticing a problem early, starting an improvement, or acting without being told.
- Strong answers should show independent judgment, action, and meaningful outcome.
- Weak answers may describe routine work rather than real initiative.
- Average answers may show some proactivity but limited impact or ownership.

Realism rules:
- Include weak, average, and strong answers.
- Include short, medium, and long answers.
- Some answers should be slightly messy or informal.
- Do not make every response polished or high-impact.
- Keep examples realistic for school projects, internships, clubs, and junior work.

Scoring guidance:
- weak:
  - clarity 2-4
  - structure 2-4
  - impact 1-3
- average:
  - clarity 5-7
  - structure 4-6
  - impact 4-6
- strong:
  - clarity 7-10
  - structure 7-10
  - impact 7-10

Missing-field guidance:
- Use realistic missing items such as:
  - "problem identification"
  - "ownership"
  - "specific actions"
  - "result"
  - "metrics"
  - "decision rationale"

Formatting rules:
- Output valid JSON only.
- Keep category exactly "initiative".
- Ensure every record includes all fields.
- Ensure "missing" is always an array.
- Do not include explanations outside the JSON.
