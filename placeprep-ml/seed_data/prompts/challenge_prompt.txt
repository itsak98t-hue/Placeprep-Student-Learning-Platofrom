Create behavioral interview dataset entries for the "challenge" category.

The goal is to generate realistic challenge-related interview responses for the PlacePrep behavioral evaluation dataset.

Use this exact JSON schema for every entry:
{
  "id": integer,
  "question": string,
  "category": "challenge",
  "answer": string,
  "label": "weak" | "average" | "strong",
  "score_clarity": integer,
  "score_structure": integer,
  "score_impact": integer,
  "missing": [string, ...]
}

Question style:
- Use realistic prompts such as:
  - "Tell me about a challenging situation you faced."
  - "Describe a difficult problem and how you handled it."
  - "Tell me about a time a task felt especially hard."

Category-specific realism:
- Focus on difficult tasks, technical blockers, unclear requirements, resource constraints, or learning curves.
- Strong answers should show persistence, problem-solving, and concrete outcomes.
- Weak answers may be too vague, skip the real obstacle, or fail to explain the resolution.
- Average answers may explain the situation but lack impact or reflection.

Realism rules:
- Include weak, average, and strong answers.
- Include short, medium, and long answers.
- Some answers should sound natural and imperfect.
- Do not make every answer highly polished.
- Keep scenarios realistic for academic, internship, and junior professional contexts.

Scoring guidance:
- weak:
  - clarity 2-4
  - structure 2-4
  - impact 1-3
- average:
  - clarity 5-7
  - structure 4-6
  - impact 4-6
- strong:
  - clarity 7-10
  - structure 7-10
  - impact 7-10

Missing-field guidance:
- Use realistic missing items such as:
  - "challenge details"
  - "specific actions"
  - "decision rationale"
  - "result"
  - "metrics"
  - "reflection"

Formatting rules:
- Output valid JSON only.
- Keep category exactly "challenge".
- Ensure every record includes all fields.
- Ensure "missing" is always an array.
- Do not include explanations outside the JSON.
